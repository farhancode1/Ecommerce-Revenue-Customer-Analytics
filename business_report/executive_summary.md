# Executive Summary

## Business question

How can management improve revenue quality, customer retention, and service performance using Olist marketplace transaction data?

## Headline findings

- The delivered-order population contains **96,478 orders** from **93,358 unique customers**, generating **R$13.22M** in merchandise revenue.
- Customer retention is weak: only **3.00%** of customers made more than one delivered purchase.
- Delivery reliability is closely associated with satisfaction: on-time orders average **4.29/5**, while late orders average **2.57/5**.
- **46.21%** of late deliveries receive a one-star review, compared with **6.59%** of on-time deliveries.
- January–August 2018 merchandise revenue was approximately **141.1% higher** than the same months in 2017.
- Health & beauty, watches & gifts, and bed/bath/table together contributed **25.9%** of merchandise revenue.
- São Paulo generated **38.3%** of merchandise revenue.
- Credit cards accounted for **78.5%** of recorded payment value.

## Recommended actions

**Retention:** focus lifecycle campaigns on converting first-time buyers into second-time buyers, with 30/60/90-day reactivation monitoring.

**Delivery:** create seller/state/category late-delivery scorecards and investigate segments with both high late-delivery rates and low review scores.

**Commercial mix:** monitor the leading categories for availability and seller performance while identifying growth opportunities outside the largest categories and São Paulo.

**Dashboard governance:** track the same KPI definitions consistently across SQL, Python, Power BI, and management reporting.

## Limitation

This is historical marketplace data from 2016–2018. The analysis demonstrates data-analytics methods and business reasoning; it is not a statement about present-day Olist or Brazilian e-commerce performance.
