# Results

These are compact, version-controlled outputs from the uploaded Olist dataset. The full reproducible pipeline in `python/build_analysis_tables.py` can regenerate the complete processed tables.

- `kpi_summary.csv` — executive KPIs
- `monthly_kpis.csv` — monthly trend table
- `top_categories.csv` — top 10 categories by merchandise revenue
- `top_states.csv` — top 10 customer states by merchandise revenue
- `payment_mix.csv` — payment method summary
- `rfm_segment_summary.csv` — RFM segment-level results
- `service_quality_summary.csv` — review-score comparison for on-time vs late deliveries
